Hiring FAQ

Answers to Common Recruiting Queries

Have questions about physical safety, scheduling shifts, relocations, or compensation cycles? Here are standard answers from our human resources team.

Ask HR Directly

Our recruiting loop usually spans 2 to 3 weeks. After submitting your application, our HR staff reviews it within 3 business days. If aligned, we schedule an introductory phone screening, followed by technical assessment and team manager interviews.

For engineering, design, HR, IT, and financial roles, we offer hybrid structures (typically 2 days remote, 3 days on-site at HQ). For our precision assembly operators, metallurgy technicians, and warehouse specialists, roles are fully on-site to ensure optimal machinery management.

Every on-floor worker undergoes a fully paid 2-week safety training program covering heavy hardware management, fire safeguards, hazard communication, and PPE. We also sponsor certifications like OSHA-10, OSHA-30, AWS, and Fanuc robotics programming.

Yes. For senior engineering, management, and highly specialized technical roles (such as CNC Programmers or Metallurgy Leads) requiring relocation to our Houston/Pasadena facilities, we offer comprehensive relocation packages.

As a global manufacturing firm with operational plants in Germany, Japan, and the United States, we support internal cross-facility transfer requests after 12 months of high-performance ratings, subject to business needs.
